# Design document and research journal · reference structures

These two short, reusable structures are for your own project. Today's slides show how they change during one continuous research example.

- [Design document](design-document.md): the **current design**. Revise the relevant sections as understanding and choices change.
- [Research journal](research-journal.md): a **record of thinking and work over time**. Add entries as you investigate, including sources and your own responses.

## How they work together

1. **Think:** put your current idea in the design; record what interests you and AI suggestions in the journal.
2. **Search:** refine your design question; record actual searches and promising leads in the journal. Ask your agent to use the [scholarly-search skill](../../../.opencode/skills/scholarly-search/SKILL.md).
3. **Organise and assign:** list works and tasks for agents; record what was actually read and the source of each finding.
4. **Read and think:** check important passages yourself. Add your thoughts to the journal; **integrate** what changes your plan into the relevant part of the design. Do not just append a note to the design.
5. **Synthesise:** develop points connecting design, evidence and scholarly discussion, with a source and locator for each finding.
6. **Loop and outline:** repeat until you have enough points, then arrange them into an outline for your ten-minute presentation.

Replace the placeholders with your own work. Record what actually happened; distinguish your own ideas, an agent's suggestions and an author's claims.
